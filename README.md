# UsableDarksteel
Adds Darksteel into actual gameplay and restores its intended appearance

You must have the third smelter gem in order to make and use Darksteel, its recipe is 1 Valyan ingot and 1 Palladium ingot

Currently, I have left its stats as they are set in vanilla (Literally just Valyan but half the weight), if enough people request it, I am open to adjusting this (or, feel free to make a fork of the mod to do this)
